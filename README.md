One sentence, at most 200 characters, pitching a bizarre startup idea that
sounds absurd until you realise it would actually sell.

**Concept: DOG DATE-JUDGES.** Singles pay to bring a trained dog on a first
date as a character judge — one growl ends the evening before months of
heartbreak, cheaper than therapy, and the dog never lies.

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes that one sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.
